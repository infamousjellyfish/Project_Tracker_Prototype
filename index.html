Milestone Framework — Critical Path

A "Delayed" status on a critical-path milestone carries higher urgency than the same status on a milestone with float.
Complete Current stage Upcoming
On Track = within ±5% of plan · At Risk = 5–15% behind plan or pace below requirement · Delayed = more than 15% behind plan. Delays typically cluster around Procurement and Deployment — the stages with highest external-stakeholder dependency.
